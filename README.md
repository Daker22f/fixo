# FIXO

https://fixo-landing.vercel.app/

FIXO is a token intelligence platform for people who follow crypto markets. It
brings market data, trader discovery, community and on-chain research together
in one place, so you can follow what is happening without jumping between tools.

## What FIXO offers

- **Market overview** — prices, trends and the tokens people are talking about.
- **Trader discovery** — find traders, see who they are and follow the ones you trust.
- **Community** — join communities, follow people and take part in discussions.
- **On-chain research** — explore tokens and wallets with live market context.
- **Watchlist and portfolio** — keep the tokens and positions you care about in one place.
- **X feed** — read what the community is posting without leaving FIXO.

## The landing page

This is the public front door of FIXO. It introduces the product and owns the
sign-in and sign-up experience. After signing in, you are taken to the app,
where your account, watchlists and follows live.

## Accounts

FIXO uses real accounts. The market figures, traders and activity you see come
from live data — there are no demo profiles or inflated counters. Create an
account with an email address or continue with a linked provider.

## Getting started

1. Open the landing page and create an account or sign in.
2. Explore the market from the dashboard.
3. Add tokens to your watchlist and follow traders or communities.
4. Use the X feed and community discussions to keep up with the market.
